Twenty on your Mac (Apple chip)

This app is not signed. macOS will ask before it runs. That is expected.

1. Unzip the download if Finder did not already.
2. Find Twenty.app.
3. Hold Control and click Twenty.app. Choose Open.
4. Click Open in the dialog.
   If you only see a message that the app cannot be opened: open System Settings, go to Privacy & Security, scroll down, and click Open Anyway. Then try Control-click Open once more.
   If it still will not open: open Terminal, type
   xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine
   then a space, then drag Twenty.app onto the Terminal window, and press Return.
   That line does not contain your API key. Do not type the key there.
5. When macOS asks for the camera, click Allow.
   If you missed it: System Settings → Privacy & Security → Camera → turn Twenty on.
6. Read the first screen. Click “I understand — keep going”.
7. Paste your Presage API key into the box labeled Presage API key.
   Click “Save key on this Mac”.
   The box hides the key. Twenty does not show it again.
   Do not paste the key into Terminal, chat, email, or a screenshot.
8. Click Solo demo to try it on your own, or Start a room and read the four-character code to the other person.

You can skip the key. Solo demo will say the numbers are practice signals and the camera stays off.

To remove the key later: on the home screen, click “Remove saved key”.

The camera light is on only while the header says Camera on. Stop camera turns it off.
