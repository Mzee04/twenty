# Demo script

About two and a half minutes. Record on the Mac with a real API key. Do not show `.env` or the key. If a second person is in the room, get their okay before they are on camera. The break screen must not reveal who triggered it.

You can cut the twenty-minute wait. Say that you are cutting it. Do not speed the timer up in the build and present it as the real pause.

## 0:00 – 0:25 Consent

Open Twenty. The header says **Camera off**.

Say:

> This is Twenty. It is a pause for a conversation, not a health app. The camera is still off. This screen says what stays on my laptop — the video, my pulse, my breathing — and what can leave: a room code, and a yes or no that someone needs a break. It does not say who.

Click **I understand — keep going**.

## 0:25 – 0:50 Two rooms, or say you are about to

If you have a second laptop:

> We each run this. I start a room. The code is all she needs. Her pulse never comes to me.

Click **Start a room**. When the camera indicator flips to **Camera on**, point at it. Read the code. On the other laptop, join. Show **Two people in this room** on both, and that neither screen shows the other person’s number.

If you are alone, use **Solo demo** and say so in the first sentence:

> This is the solo demo. The other person is a simulated recording. My camera is live. The screen keeps saying that, so it is not a hidden second person.

Leave **Demo partner stays calm** unchecked if you want the automatic cue later. For a faster two-machine shot, use **Timeout** and do not wait for the cue.

## 0:50 – 1:25 Live signal

Stay in frame, with light on your face. When a number appears:

> That number is my pulse estimate from Presage’s SDK, a 12-second average. It stays on this computer. The first seconds are often blank while that window fills. If I turn away, it pauses instead of guessing.

Turn your face out of frame for a moment. The hint and the **Paused** dot should show. Turn back.

> Talking is fine for the pulse. Breathing is a bad signal while we talk, so Twenty does not use it to call the break.

## 1:25 – 1:50 The break

Either:

- Press **Timeout** on one machine, or
- In solo demo, wait until the simulated partner’s trace holds high. That is a calm minute, then a rise, then about twenty seconds. Roughly two minutes all together. You can cut the wait and come back as the color changes.

When both screens (or the one demo screen) change:

> Same color on both sides. “Someone needs a break.” No name. Twenty minutes, and a breath guide. If my breathing rate is confident and I am sitting quietly, the guide follows it, a little slower. If not, it is six breaths a minute.

Point at **In** / **Out** and the timer reading **20:00** or just under.

## 1:50 – 2:20 Ready, and the camera

Tap **I'm ready** on one screen.

> I am ready. It still will not open until she is ready and the twenty minutes are gone. That wait is the point. I am not going to sit here for the whole twenty in this video.

If this is the solo demo, click **Mark demo partner ready** and say that the button exists only because the partner is simulated.

Click **Stop camera**. The indicator returns to **Camera off**. The preview goes blank.

> The camera is off. The guide falls back to a steady pace. Nothing was saved.

## 2:20 – 2:40 Close

> Raw pulse never left the laptop. The relay only heard “break” and “ready.” Telemetry from the SDK is off. There is no account and no AI. It is a cue to step away, and either of us can call it without a reading.

Leave the room, or quit. End on the consent line in the footer: wellness cue only, not a medical device.

## What has to be true in the shot

- Camera-on indicator, then camera off after **Stop camera**.
- A changing pulse number from a live session, with the “stays on this computer” caption.
- The break line **Someone needs a break** on the shared color `#e6d2c3`.
- If two machines: both on that color, and neither showing the other person’s pulse.
- If solo: the sentence that the partner is simulated, visible on screen.

## What not to do

- Do not film the API key, the `.env` file, or a Presage dashboard token.
- Do not claim the number is a diagnosis, a stress score, or proof that someone is flooded.
- Do not hide that a solo partner is simulated.
